Detailed Services

Explore how our specialized facility management services can benefit your organization.

Hard Services

Our hard services are focused on the physical structure and systems of your building. We provide comprehensive technical maintenance including HVAC, electrical systems, plumbing, and overall mechanical systems management. Our certified technicians ensure that your building's core infrastructure operates efficiently, safely, and compliantly, minimizing downtime and extending the lifespan of your critical assets.

  • ✓ HVAC Maintenance & Repair
  • ✓ Electrical Systems Management
  • ✓ Plumbing & Water Systems

Soft Services

Soft services are essential for creating a comfortable, safe, and welcoming environment for your occupants. Sator Services offers top-tier cleaning, dedicated security personnel, professional catering, and meticulous landscaping. We tailor these support services to match the exact needs of your facility, ensuring daily operations run smoothly and your brand's image is impeccably maintained.

  • ✓ Professional Cleaning & Janitorial
  • ✓ Security & Access Control
  • ✓ Landscaping & Grounds Maintenance

Integrated Facility Management (IFM)

For organizations looking for a seamless, single-point-of-contact solution, our Integrated FM services combine both hard and soft services under one unified management structure. This approach eliminates the friction of dealing with multiple vendors, drives cost efficiencies through shared resources, and ensures all facility operations are aligned with your overarching business goals.

  • ✓ Single Point of Contact
  • ✓ Cost Optimization
  • ✓ Unified Reporting & Strategy

Project Management

Whether you are undertaking a minor office refurbishment or a major facility upgrade, our project management team delivers end-to-end oversight. We handle planning, procurement, contractor management, and quality assurance to ensure that your facility projects are completed on time, within budget, and to the highest industry standards.

  • ✓ Refurbishments & Renovations
  • ✓ Contractor Management
  • ✓ Budget & Timeline Adherence

Business Excellence (QHSE)

Compliance and safety are at the core of effective facility management. Our Business Excellence division implements robust Quality, Health, Safety, and Environmental (QHSE) management systems. We conduct regular audits, risk assessments, and compliance checks to guarantee that your facility meets all local regulations and international standards, protecting both your people and your reputation.

  • ✓ Health & Safety Compliance
  • ✓ Quality Assurance Audits
  • ✓ Environmental Management

Disinfection Services

In today's health-conscious climate, maintaining a sanitary environment is more critical than ever. We provide professional-grade disinfection and sanitization services using approved chemicals and advanced techniques (such as fogging). Our proactive and reactive disinfection protocols ensure your facility remains a safe, healthy space for employees and visitors alike.

  • ✓ Deep Cleaning & Sanitization
  • ✓ Fogging & Electrostatic Spraying
  • ✓ Epidemic Prevention Protocols
